Phase 1 — Foundation

Website Audit: SEO, Content & AI Optimization

Before any campaign goes live, the website needs to be the strongest possible foundation. Current state of sumpro.in reveals several high-priority gaps.

⚙️ Technical SEO Issues Critical

  • Footer social media links point to # — not real profiles. Social signals missing entirely
  • Homepage shows contradictory numbers: "50+ enterprise clients" vs "15+ Enterprise Clients" vs "15+ Happy Clients" — damages credibility & E-E-A-T
  • Meta description is generic; needs keyword-intent alignment per page
  • All pages share identical meta keywords — zero page-level SEO differentiation
  • No structured data (Schema.org) for Organization, Service, FAQ, or LocalBusiness — missed AI Overview triggers
  • Canonical tags all point to root domain — sub-pages need proper canonical setup
  • No blog-level article schema; reduces chances of appearing in SERP rich results

📄 On-Page & Content Quality High Priority

  • Homepage H1: "IT Software Development Company | Cloud, HR, SEO Services" — good keyword density but weak brand narrative hook
  • Service pages lack detailed content — thin copy doesn't signal topical depth to Google or LLMs
  • Blog section exists but appears unpopulated or thin — major missed SEO & authority opportunity
  • No case study or results-backed content anywhere on site — critical for B2B trust
  • Portfolio page exists but without detailed project narratives, measurable outcomes, or client industry tags
  • Internal linking structure is minimal — no topic clusters or pillar-page architecture evident

🤖 AEO / GEO Optimization Immediate Action

  • No FAQ sections on service pages — FAQ schema is the #1 driver for AI Overview citations
  • No direct-answer content blocks ("What is [service]?", "How does cloud migration work?") — LLMs can't extract clean answers
  • No structured comparison content (e.g., sumPRO vs typical alternatives) that AI tools love to reference
  • Zero use of concise definition paragraphs at page tops — required for featured snippets & ChatGPT/Gemini citations
  • Content lacks "people also ask" targeting — these queries are now directly mapped to AI answers
  • No author bios or team expertise pages — Google's E-E-A-T requires demonstrated human expertise signals

🔧 Immediate Action Plan — Before Any Ad Spend

Fix social profile links → Add Organization + LocalBusiness + Service schema to all pages → Add FAQ schema to every service page → Write 400+ word content blocks per service → Standardize all social proof numbers → Set up GA4 + GSC → Create author profile pages for key team members. This audit work will multiply the ROI of every subsequent campaign.

Phase 2 — Organic Growth

SEO & AEO/GEO Strategy

Dual-track search strategy — traditional SEO for long-term rankings, AEO/GEO to capture AI-driven discovery through ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

🔍 Core SEO Strategy
  • Build pillar-cluster architecture: one pillar page per service (e.g., "Software Development Services in India"), supported by 5–8 cluster blogs targeting long-tail keywords
  • Target high-intent, low-competition keywords: "IT company Gurugram", "custom ERP development India", "cloud migration AWS India", "HR payroll outsourcing startup"
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile setup, local citations, location-tagged service pages
  • Technical: XML sitemap, proper robots.txt, page speed optimization, Core Web Vitals
  • Backlink strategy: guest posts on Indian tech/startup publications (YourStory, Inc42, Entrepreneur India), industry directories, NASSCOM listings
  • Monthly GSC + GA4 audit: track impressions, CTR, clicks per page, and fix crawl errors
  • Tools: SEMrush, Ahrefs, Screaming Frog, Google Search Console, GA4
🤖 AEO / GEO Strategy (AI Search)
  • Add FAQ schema to every service page with 6–8 Q&A pairs answering specific buyer questions (e.g., "How much does cloud migration cost in India?")
  • Write "definition-first" content blocks: every page opens with a clear, citable 2–3 sentence definition of the service
  • Create dedicated "What is [X]?" educational articles targeting queries that AI tools pull from (e.g., "What is ERP software?", "What is a Healthcare IT system?")
  • Comparison content: "sumPRO vs Freelance Developers — Which Is Right for Your Startup?" — this format is heavily cited by AI tools
  • Structured data: HowTo schema for technical process pages, Review schema for testimonials
  • Submit XML sitemap to Bing (used by Copilot) and ensure robots.txt allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, GoogleBot-Extended
  • Tools: Schema Markup Generator, AlsoAsked, AnswerThePublic, Perplexity monitoring

Phase 3 — Content Engine

Content Strategy & Audience Engagement

Content is sumPRO's most scalable asset. A structured three-pillar approach builds organic traffic, demonstrates expertise, and nurtures leads through every funnel stage.

📚 Layer 1 — Educational (TOFU)
  • "What is Cloud Migration? A Guide for Indian SMBs"
  • "ERP vs Custom Software: What Does Your Startup Actually Need?"
  • "GST Registration Process in India — Step by Step"
  • "How to Choose an IT Partner for Your Healthcare Clinic"
  • "Understanding Structured Cabling for Data Centres"
  • Format: Long-form (1500–2500 words), FAQ schema, featured image, AI-answer optimized
⚖️ Layer 2 — Comparative (MOFU)
  • "sumPRO vs Hiring an In-House IT Team: Full Cost Breakdown"
  • "AWS vs Azure vs Google Cloud — Which Is Right for Your Business?"
  • "5 Signs Your Business Is Ready for a Custom ERP"
  • "Freelancer vs Agency for Digital Marketing — Pros, Cons & Pricing"
  • "Top 7 IT Companies for Startups in India (2026)"
  • Format: Comparison tables, expert POV, internal links to service pages
🏆 Layer 3 — Proof (BOFU)
  • Client case studies with before/after metrics (even if anonymized initially)
  • "How We Built an ERP for [Industry] in 6 Weeks" — project narratives
  • Founder thought leadership: Nandini's perspective on India's digital transformation
  • Team expertise blogs: "From Telecom to Healthcare IT — How Our Team Bridges Industries"
  • Testimonial deep-dives: expanded client quotes with context
  • Format: Long-form narrative, quote callouts, schema-marked reviews

📅 Content Calendar: 90-Day Sprint

Month 1: 8 Layer 1 educational articles (2 per service) + FAQ optimization of all service pages.
Month 2: 6 Layer 2 comparison posts + 2 case study drafts + LinkedIn content calendar live.
Month 3: 4 Layer 3 proof pieces + 2 lead magnet resources published + blog newsletter sequence (6 emails). Target: 40+ indexed pages by end of Month 3.

Phase 3 — Paid Growth

Google Ads & Meta Paid Campaign Strategy

Paid campaigns bridge the gap while SEO matures. Every rupee must be accountable — no brand awareness campaigns until conversion infrastructure is proven.

🟢

Google Search Campaigns

Target high-intent buyers actively searching for sumPRO's exact services.

  • Campaign 1: Software Development (ERP, Web Dev, Mobile App)
  • Campaign 2: Cloud Services (AWS/Azure migration, setup)
  • Campaign 3: Digital Marketing (SEO, PPC, Social Media)
  • Campaign 4: HR & Compliance (payroll, GST, company registration)
  • Match types: Phrase + Exact only in Month 1 — control spend
  • Negative keywords: "free", "DIY", "tutorial", "jobs" — essential for B2B
  • Ad extensions: Sitelinks, Callouts, Call, Location
🔵

Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)

Reach decision-makers and business owners before they actively search — awareness to consideration stage.

  • Audience: Business owners + startup founders, 25–50 age, India, interests: startup, B2B software, business growth
  • Retargeting: Website visitors (14-day window) → lead magnet offer
  • Lead Gen Ads: Directly capture name + email + company without leaving Facebook
  • Carousel ads: Service showcase ("6 ways sumPRO can grow your business")
  • Video ads: 15–30 second founder intro video — highest trust builder
  • Lookalike audiences from email list once 200+ subscribers
🟡

Campaign Infrastructure (Pre-Launch)

Non-negotiables before spending a rupee on ads.

  • Google Tag Manager setup: track all form submissions, CTA clicks, scroll depth
  • GA4 conversion events: Contact form, WhatsApp click, phone call, portfolio view
  • Meta Pixel: Install on all pages, verify with Events Manager, create Custom Conversions
  • Dedicated landing pages per campaign — no sending traffic to homepage
  • UTM parameters on every ad URL — mandatory for attribution
  • A/B test: 2 headlines, 2 CTAs, 2 audience segments — always running

💰 Recommended Starting Budget

Month 1–2: ₹15,000–25,000/month total. 60% Google Search (higher intent), 40% Meta (awareness + retargeting). Optimize for cost per lead (CPL) under ₹500. Scale the channels hitting CPL targets. Month 3+: Double budget on winning campaigns, pause losers.

Phase 4 — Pipeline Building

Lead Generation Strategy

Multi-channel lead gen combining gated tools, outbound outreach, strategic partnerships, and conversion-optimized website elements. Goal: build a pipeline of 20–30 qualified leads/month by Month 3.

🔒 Gated Resource Tools (Lead Magnets)

01

IT Project Cost Estimator

An interactive calculator where users input project type (web app, ERP, mobile), team size, timeline, and tech stack preferences — and get an estimated project cost range + recommendation. High-value, highly shareable.

📧 Email Gate 💼 B2B Intent 🏆 Lead Quality: High
02

Cloud Readiness Assessment

A 10-question audit tool that assesses a company's infrastructure, security posture, and readiness for cloud migration. Output: a personalized readiness score + a downloadable PDF report with sumPRO's recommendations.

📧 Email Gate 📄 PDF Report 🏆 Lead Quality: Very High
03

Business Compliance Checklist

A downloadable, interactive PDF covering GST, MSME, FSSAI, and company registration requirements based on business type (startup, SMB, healthcare, food). Targets sumPRO's compliance vertical — an underserved, high-intent audience.

📧 Email Gate 📥 Download ⚡ Fast to Produce
04

Free Website SEO Audit Report

Users enter their website URL and receive a basic automated SEO audit (powered by a tool like SE Ranking or Screaming Frog API). Gate it behind a form — this attracts exactly the kind of SMB who needs sumPRO's digital marketing services.

📧 Email Gate ⚙️ Automated 🎯 High Volume
